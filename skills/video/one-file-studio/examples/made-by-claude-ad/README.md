# Example: the "made entirely by Claude" ad (52-54 s, 16:9)

An ad about making ads without an editor, with a second, energetic ad about local-business websites playing inside it.
It ends on "every frame came from one prompt", a Follow + comment CTA and a character urgency gag.

## Arc

| Beat | Line (tone) | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "What if you could make ads like this... without ever touching an editor?" (curious, then soft; echo on this line only) | A real mountain photo: the camera starts inside the pink sky and pulls back to the range. A glow-orb wipe to a light page, the keyword "Editor?" in a gradient |
| No-list | "No designer. No camera. No timeline." (confident, fast) | Rows rolling through a focus band, an arrow nudging on each |
| The artifact | "Just Claude... and one prompt." (soft, warm) | A prompt card slides in left to right (whoosh) inside a cloud-photo window; selection box, cursor click |
| Process | "I opened Claude Code, typed what I wanted, and hit Enter." (casual) | A real laptop photo re-projected straight-on, floating in real clouds; the UI mapped onto its screen; push in to type; model picker; Send; halftone ripple |
| Turn | "Here's what it built me." (whisper) | Pull back, then a zoom-transition into the screen |
| Inner ad | A second voice, accented, high energy | Outdated sites glitch (short and soft), Claude app with an attached photo, "Claude is writing...", the site builds with a code panel and status chips, full-screen heroes for three trades with bracket labels, a 3D carousel, a pixel-resolve "Built with Claude." lockup |
| Reveal | "Every frame from that one video came from one prompt. I know. I didn't believe it either." (awe, then a soft laugh) | Back to the clouds; glass pills synced to the words; a night horizon glow on "I didn't believe it" |
| CTA | "Want it? Make sure you're following me... and comment PROMPT. I'll send it to your DMs." (playful, warm) | Profile card, the cursor clicks Follow → "Following ✓", PROMPT typed in the comment box, the DM with the file |
| Urgency | "The first fifty people get it free. After that... it's paid." (urgent, serious) | A pixel character builds a dot-matrix "50"; a magnifier turns FREE into PAID |

## Hook variants (tested)

**B, the flash-forward:**
- 3 s of the best shots, cut on every word of "This entire ad? Claude made it."
- "THIS ENTIRE AD?" / "CLAUDE MADE IT." on a dark glass band.
- Spliced onto the approved video at "No designer".

**C, the reorder:**
- Open on the inner ad itself, then "What if you could make ads like this...", through "and hit Enter".
- Cut to the reveal.
- Seams were chosen where the motion matched (a laptop pulling back cuts to a laptop pulling back).

Both are built without re-rendering the approved video (`references/video-render-qa.md` → Variants).
